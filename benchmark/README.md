# Historical benchmark evidence

`history/*.json` preserves measurements from the removed 0.14.0 implementation.
These files are historical evidence only; they do not measure the new binding
compiler or predict its performance. The old runners were removed with that
implementation. See `doc/history/0.14.0/performance.md` for their original context.

A new equivalent-semantics benchmark suite belongs to the cross-topic #114
conformance work once real conversion and lifecycle engines exist.
